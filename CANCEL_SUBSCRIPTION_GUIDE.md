# How to Cancel Your Subscription (Before Trial Ends)

## 🚨 Quick Fix - Cancel Now!

### Option 1: Cancel on iPhone (Easiest)

1. **Open Settings** on your iPhone
2. Tap your **name** at the top
3. Tap **"Subscriptions"** (or "Media & Purchases" → "Subscriptions")
4. Find **"Craveless"** or **"Sugar Reset"** subscription
5. Tap on it
6. Tap **"Cancel Subscription"** or **"Cancel Free Trial"**
7. Confirm cancellation

**You'll still have access until the trial ends, but won't be charged!**

---

### Option 2: Cancel via App Store

1. Open **App Store** app
2. Tap your **profile icon** (top right)
3. Tap **"Subscriptions"**
4. Find **"Craveless"** subscription
5. Tap **"Cancel Subscription"**
6. Confirm

---

### Option 3: Cancel via App Store Connect Website

1. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
2. Sign in with your Apple ID (victortomashaugaard@gmail.com)
3. Find your subscription
4. Click **"Cancel Subscription"**

---

## ✅ What Happens After Canceling

- ✅ **Trial continues** until it ends (3 days from when you started)
- ✅ **No charge** - you won't be billed
- ✅ **Access continues** until trial expires
- ✅ **Then goes to free tier** (limited features)
